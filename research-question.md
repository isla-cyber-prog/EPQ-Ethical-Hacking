# Research Question

To what extent is penetration testing the most effective way to protect companies from cyber-attacks?

## Objectives
The objectives of this project are to:

- Understand what penetration testing is and how it works.
- Investigate the role of ethical hackers in cybersecurity.
- Examine the advantages of penetration testing.
- Identify the limitations of penetration testing.
- Compare penetration testing with other cybersecurity measures.
- Analyse real-world examples of cyber-attacks.
- Evaluate whether penetration testing is the most effective method of protecting organisations.

## Rationale

I chose this topic because cybersecurity is an area that interests me and relates to my future career interests. As cyber-attacks continue to increase, organisations must find effective ways of protecting their systems and data. This project aims to evaluate whether penetration testing is the best solution compared to other security measures.
