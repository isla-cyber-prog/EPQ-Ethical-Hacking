# Research Question

## Main Research Question

To what extent is penetration testing the most effective way to protect companies from cyber-attacks?

## Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Understand what penetration testing is and how it works.
- Investigate the role of ethical hackers in cybersecurity.
- Examine the advantages of penetration testing.
- Identify the limitations of penetration testing.
- Compare penetration testing with other cybersecurity measures.
- Analyse real-world examples of cyber-attacks and security breaches.
- Evaluate whether penetration testing is the most effective method of protecting organisations.

## Rationale

I chose this topic because cybersecurity is an area of growing importance and aligns with my future academic and career interests. As cyber-attacks continue to increase, organisations must adopt effective security measures to protect their systems, networks, and sensitive data.

Penetration testing is widely used to identify vulnerabilities before they can be exploited by malicious attackers. However, organisations also rely on alternative security measures such as employee awareness training, vulnerability assessments, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and incident response planning.

This project aims to investigate whether penetration testing is the most effective method of protection or whether a combination of cybersecurity strategies provides a more effective defence against cyber threats.
