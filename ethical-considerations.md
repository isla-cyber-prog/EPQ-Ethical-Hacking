# Ethical Considerations

## Permission

Penetration testing should only be carried out with the explicit permission of the organisation or system owner. Attempting to access systems without authorisation is unethical and may be illegal.

## Responsible Disclosure

Any vulnerabilities discovered should be reported responsibly to allow organisations sufficient time to address security weaknesses before they can be exploited by malicious actors.

## Privacy

Cybersecurity activities should respect individual privacy and avoid unnecessary access to personal or confidential information. Data should be handled securely and only used for legitimate purposes.

## Legal Considerations

Relevant legislation includes:

- Computer Misuse Act 1990
- Data Protection Act 2018
- UK GDPR

These laws help regulate access to computer systems and the processing of personal data.

## Professional Conduct

Ethical hackers are expected to act professionally, legally, and responsibly at all times. Their role is to improve security rather than cause harm or gain unauthorised access.

## Research Ethics

This project is based entirely on research from publicly available and reputable sources. No unauthorised testing of systems, networks, or devices has been conducted as part of this EPQ.
