# Ethical Considerations

## Permission

Penetration testing should only be carried out with the permission of the organisation or system owner.

## Responsible Disclosure

Any vulnerabilities discovered should be reported responsibly to allow organisations to fix security weaknesses before they can be exploited.

## Privacy

Cybersecurity activities should respect individual privacy and avoid unnecessary access to personal information.

## Legal Considerations

Relevant legislation includes:

- Computer Misuse Act 1990
- Data Protection Act 2018
- UK GDPR

## Professional Conduct

Ethical hackers are expected to act professionally, legally, and responsibly at all times.
