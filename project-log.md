# Project Log

### Log 1 - Initial Ideas

## Activities Completed
- Explored possible EPQ topic areas based on personal interests and future career aspirations.
- Considered different areas within cybersecurity, including ethical hacking and digital security.
- Conducted initial research into ethical hacking and cybersecurity practices.
- Identified penetration testing as a specific area of interest within ethical hacking.
- Began reviewing academic and industry sources to assess the scope and relevance of the topic.
- Developed an initial research question: "To what extent is penetration testing the most effective way to protect companies from cyberattacks?"
- Planned potential research methods, including analysis of academic literature, industry reports, and practical investigation through TryHackMe activities.

### Reflection

Exploring different topic areas helped me identify cybersecurity as a subject that genuinely interests me and links to my future academic and career interests. My original idea was to research ethical hacking more broadly, but after completing some background reading I realised that ethical hacking covers many different areas. As a result, I narrowed my focus to penetration testing because it is one of the most widely used methods organisations employ to identify vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.

Initial research showed that penetration testing connects to several interesting themes, including cybercrime, legal and ethical considerations, and organisational security strategies. This helped me develop a more focused research question that allows for evaluation and analysis rather than simple description. I also identified a potential challenge: ensuring that penetration testing is assessed fairly alongside other cybersecurity approaches such as continuous monitoring and employee security awareness training. Overall, this stage helped me refine my project into a focused and manageable investigation while identifying possible directions for future research.

### Log 2 - Candidate Proposal (Part A)

### Activities Completed
- Refined my project focus from ethical hacking in general to penetration testing.
- Developed the research question: "To what extent is penetration testing the most effective way to protect companies from cyber-attacks?"
- Identified a range of academic, industry and research sources to support the investigation.
- Evaluated different perspectives on penetration testing, including technical, practical, legal and ethical viewpoints.
- Planned the main areas of investigation, including the effectiveness, limitations and challenges of penetration testing.
- Decided to include practical research through TryHackMe activities.
- Planned to analyse data from the Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR) to compare common cyber-attacks with the capabilities of penetration testing.
- Determined that the final product would be a 5,000-word research essay.

### Reflection

Completing the candidate proposal helped me clarify both the direction and scope of my project. My original interest was ethical hacking as a whole, but research showed that this topic was too broad for a focused investigation. As a result, I narrowed my focus to penetration testing and developed a research question that encourages evaluation rather than description.

Reviewing a range of academic and industry sources helped me identify different viewpoints on the effectiveness of penetration testing. Some sources present it as an essential cybersecurity measure, while others argue that it must be combined with approaches such as continuous monitoring, access controls and security awareness training. This highlighted the importance of maintaining a balanced perspective throughout the project.

Planning my research methods also helped me understand how I will gather evidence. Alongside academic literature, I intend to use practical TryHackMe activities and analyse data from the Verizon DBIR to compare real-world cyber threats with the vulnerabilities that penetration testing can identify. Moving forward, I will focus on expanding my research and evaluating evidence from multiple sources to reach a well-supported conclusion.
